# SFX study: Notion concept ad (N1)
A 33.6 s, 1280×720 film with no voiceover, music at 136 BPM, mixed at −18.8 LUFS. It is almost one continuous take,
with only two scene cuts. Measured by splitting the soundtrack into bands and finding the peaks in each.
- **25 soft mid-band clicks or pops:**
  - one per word as the line builds ("Your / work / is / everywhere" at 0.44, 0.63, 0.70, 0.86, 1.06 s)
  - on UI elements popping in
  - on the typing in the end card
- **22 low sub-thumps (under 150 Hz),** about one every 1.2 s: under nearly every card or panel landing, the logo, and each
  feature card.
- **11 high-band air swooshes:**
  - short passes of 0.1–0.3 s on most transitions
  - longer swirls of up to 0.75 s at the big reveals (the 2.55 s "Meet Notion" swap; the 7.8–8.5 s beach and calendar tilt)
- **Mix:** all of it sits under the music; the sounds are felt more than heard. There is no single loud effect.
- **Rule taken from it:** every word lands with a pop, every landing gets a sub-thump, and every transition gets an air pass.
  Some moves are left silent so it never feels like a sound per frame.
