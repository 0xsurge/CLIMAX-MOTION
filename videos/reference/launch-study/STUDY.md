# Launch film study: six reference videos

Source: six clips the user sent as the taste reference for product launch films, plus the isaac.edits
"3-second hook rule" post. All are 736x414 at 30 fps. The clips are kept locally in `v/` (git-ignored).
Contact sheets come from `sheets/` (1 fps and 10 fps; regenerate with the commands at the end).

| id | product | length | ground | sound | cuts found |
|----|---------|--------|--------|-------|------------|
| V1 | Synaps, an AI canvas for architects | 80 s | black, glossy blue (later orange) 3D ribbons | music only, 117 BPM, -15 LUFS | 5 (almost one take) |
| V2 | lovio sting | 7.7 s | blurred photo, then a blue-violet gradient | silent | 1 |
| V3 | ProductHype, a video agency | 29 s | black/teal/lime gradient, white UI | voiceover + music, 103 BPM, -15 LUFS | 27 (fast) |
| V4 | "SaaS gradient" transition pack demo | 42 s | surreal AI landscapes | music, 99 BPM, -16 LUFS | 9, every 3.3 s |
| V5 | Paage, AI commerce pages | 70 s | white with pastel aurora fog | soft music only, 117 BPM, -24 LUFS | 17 |
| V6 | Magic Animator, a Figma plugin | 71 s | landscape wallpaper desktop, bright UI | voiceover + music, 103 BPM, -16 LUFS | 44 (montage bursts) |

## V1, Synaps (the strongest reference)
- **Ground.** 83% of pixels are near black (#030404). Behind the type, glossy chrome-blue 3D ribbons and
  folded tubes drift slowly, always out of focus at the edges. A middle section turns the same ribbons
  red-orange (#E8501E), so colour marks the chapter.
- **Word-by-word captions.** One short line, centred, about 5% of frame height, white, medium weight.
  - Each word arrives in about 3 frames: from heavy blur (about 12 px) and a small offset to sharp.
  - The next word starts before the last has settled: about 0.15 to 0.3 s per word.
  - The whole line keeps creeping, slightly shrinking or drifting left, while it reads.
  - Exit: the line blurs and slides off in about 3 frames. The next line is already arriving.
  - The key word takes the accent colour (electric blue gradient, about #3B82F6 to #93C5FD).
- **Slam words.** Every 6 to 10 s, one giant word ("Meet", "Instantly", "Introducing", "Real") fills 13 to
  30% of frame height.
  - It enters from a zoom blur, scale about 1.3 settling to 1.
  - It keeps shrinking slowly (about 10% a second) until a hard cut on the beat.
  - "Introducing" enters with wide letter-spacing that closes while it zooms.
- **Logo build.**
  - The symbol slams in big and blurred, then shrinks to size over the ribbons.
  - The wordmark letters fly in from scattered positions (s, y, n, a, p, s each start somewhere different)
    and snap into the line one by one, about 0.1 s apart.
- **Word swap.** "No more [modeling / texturing / lighting]": the first half stays put. The swapped word
  streaks in sideways with a blue motion-smear trail, holds about 0.5 s, then the next streaks over it.
- **Text made with the product.** "Design together": a collaborator cursor with a name pill (Sarah,
  orange) drags a selection box, and the word types into it letter by letter. A second cursor (Robert,
  blue) joins. The product's own interaction writes the headline.
- **The UI.**
  - **Seen whole:** the real dark product UI, tilted in perspective (about 15 to 25 degrees), on a slow dolly.
  - **Macro shots:** one button, a measurement field, a cursor drawing a wall, a prompt being typed.
    The UI is never shown flat and whole for long.
  - **Big:** prompts type at a large size, and the send button is clicked with a press.
- **Outputs.** Generated images arrive as a cascade of tilted cards in 3D depth.
- **End.** The logo, then a small support line: "Join thousands using Synaps today".
- **Pacing.** Something new every 0.5 to 1 s.
- **Sound.** The music has two drops in energy (24 to 31 s and 58 to 66 s, down to about 25%). They
  give the film room to breathe before the next chapter. Cuts land on the beat or the half beat.

## V2, lovio (sting)
- A blurred landscape photo behind a thin white grid. A caption types in a frosted box: "More about what's next".
- A soft horizontal light band wipes the frame (a motivated light sweep), and the world turns blue-violet.
- "create today" settles in a rounded geometric sans.
- A 3D iridescent flower blooms to full frame, then folds down into the small logo mark. The logo locks
  up with the wordmark on navy (#060B18).
- One object becomes the logo: the flower IS the mark.

## V3, ProductHype
- **Hook.** "Let's be" types on a black-to-teal-to-lime gradient. Then real footage of bored people at
  laptops: the problem, shown as people.
- **Stacked windows.** Footage and illustrations collide as overlapping windows, which reads as chaos.
- **Product.** Real product UI on white, steeply tilted (30 to 45 degrees), on fast push-ins.
  - Shots change with whip zooms and page-curl peels: the UI page curls away to show the next one.
  - Motion blur on every fast move.
- **Cursor as hero.** A huge 3D cursor clicks a lime pill button ("Get a demo"). The button fills the
  frame and becomes a lime ground, and "Now" lands on it in black.
- **Cuts.** Cuts land on the beat (median 0.08 s off).
- **Voiceover.** A conversational voiceover drives it, and captions repeat key words only.
- **End.** Logo "P/H" with a lime slash, then the wordmark and a tagline.

## V4, "SaaS gradient" transitions
- Surreal, saturated AI landscapes, each a single held object (a chair on a flower hill, a hammock over
  a pink fur hill, tulips against blue sky, a retro computer on grass).
- **Transitions.** Gradient-map washes: the new shot arrives as a duotone (purple, pink, gold or yellow
  gradient map) and the true colour resolves through it. Dissolves also run through halftone dots,
  pixel dither and soft blur.
- **Shots.** Each shot holds about 3.3 s with a slow push.
- **Multiples.** A grid of 9 and a strip of 5 show the range.

## V5, Paage
- **Ground.** White (#F9F7F9) with pastel aurora fog (pink #F2D7F2, peach #FAE9D6, lilac #D0D0F5, mint/cyan),
  drifting slowly. Very soft and airy.
- **Type.**
  - Support lines are small, dark and centred ("Selling", "as simple as talking").
  - Statements are huge white bold words inside a coloured fog band ("Used to be", "any", "Every Day").
    They drift sideways, too big for the frame, and get clipped at the edges on purpose.
- **Archive photo.** One black-and-white archive photo (a market seller) says "it used to be simple".
- **Product.**
  - A prompt bar on the fog; a product photo drops in; a long prompt types at a big size.
  - The cursor clicks send and the real result page builds. The camera follows the cursor through real UI.
  - "Analysing / Benchmarking / Finalising" status lines tick by beside an iridescent orb.
- **Laptop.** A laptop mockup in hard striped window shadow: a photographic beat between UI scenes.
- **Numbers.** 109,094 rolls to 110,000 in a giant black italic, with a glowing line drawing under it.
- **Who it's for.** Photo cards of users float in depth around a word that changes ("Florists",
  "Photographers", "Yoga teachers", "Restaurants").
- **End.** On pure white: "Create yours on paage". The cursor sweeps the logo and it lights up in
  gradient colours, then it locks up black.
- **Sound.** Quiet music (-24 LUFS), no voiceover, gentle.

## V6, Magic Animator
- **Desktop.** A real desktop (a landscape wallpaper). A folder opens, and windows fly out and assemble
  into the Figma workspace.
- **UI moves.** UI cards tilt into 3D over a bright blue (#1E5BFF) grid with ripple rings. A floating
  "Generating animations" pill appears.
- **Montage bursts.** Card variations cycle every 4 frames (11.6 to 13.4 s): the plugin card stays fixed,
  and the brand world behind and inside it swaps (IBM, Figma, Slack, Louis Vuitton, Wrapped...).
- **Pause.** A white pause card: "Animate in seconds", a small logo spark and a cursor.
- **Real feature shots.** Easing curve editor, timeline, export menu (GIF / MP4 / Lottie), code snippets.
- **Voiceover.** A conversational voiceover.
- **End.** A white card and a URL pill with a gradient-stroke glow, "magicanimator.com".

## The hook rule (isaac.edits post)
The first 3 seconds need three things, because most people watch without sound:
1. text on screen
2. a visual element that grabs attention
3. a bold statement

## What runs through all six
1. **Type is the narrator.** One sans family. Short lines (2 to 5 words). Built word by word or letter
   by letter, never all at once.
2. **Two type scales only.**
   - Caption: about 4 to 6% of frame height.
   - Slam word: 13 to 35% of frame height, sometimes bigger than the frame.
3. **Every entrance is blur to sharp,** with a small offset or zoom. Nothing just fades.
4. **The real product appears in close-ups and tilted planes:** macro crops, steep perspective, a cursor
   doing a real task, prompts typed big.
5. **A cursor is a character.** It clicks, drags, types, sweeps the logo. Collaborator name pills count too.
6. **The ground has a mood:** a dark void with glossy 3D forms, pastel aurora fog on white, or lush
   generated worlds. Colour changes mark chapters.
7. **Constant motion.** Lines creep, words shrink after a slam, cameras dolly. Bursts of fast montage
   (4 frames a shot) sit against calm stretches.
8. **The music leads.** Cuts on the beat or half beat. Energy dips give breathing room before a chapter.
   Two of the four with sound have no voiceover.
9. **Endings are quiet.** Logo built from its own pieces, then one small support line or URL pill.
