# Resend concept ad (M1): movement study
This is a 16.4 s, 720×720 film with no voiceover. The music runs at 129 BPM, is beat-led and stays at steady energy
(−13.7 LUFS). Its camera was measured with `camflow.py` (`flow.json`); the frames were read at 4 fps.

## Story told through the product's own UI states
"We're live 🚀" is typed and Send is clicked, but the email lands as **0 opens** with a **Spam** badge.
"Nobody saw it." Then **Re-send** is pressed, the status reads **Sending…**, the email is **Delivered**, and the screen
shows **98%** with "Reach humans, not spam folders." The close is "Want this for your launch?" with
"DM me · LAUNCH". The problem is shown as real UI failing, not described.

## Movements
- **Light into caret (0–0.5 s):** a blurred light bar flares, then sharpens into the text caret of the subject field,
  which starts typing. The first frame is a light event.
- **Button glow on press:** the cursor presses Send, the button lights up blue, and small light sparks crackle around
  its edge. The same happens on Re-send, followed by a press-down.
- **Pull-out to consequence:** after the click, the camera pulls out fast (×0.5 within 1 s). The composer shrinks
  into one row of an inbox list, where "0 opens" and the Spam badge appear.
- **Accent travelling through a line:** in "Nobody saw it.", the blue accent moves across the words as they build.
- **Button into orb into loader (carrier):** the Re-send button collapses into a glowing sphere. The sphere becomes
  the "Sending…" status dot, which then slides into a delivered row.
- **Burst into an assembled card:** emails fly out as cards in depth and assemble into a single email preview
  ("Meet Acme."), with "Delivered" chips orbiting it.
- **Number on a light pool:** "98%" lands huge on a green glow, shrinks, and lifts to make room for the caption,
  which builds word by word.
- **Avatar close plus a keyword CTA:** a memoji avatar, "Want this for your launch?", then a small pill reading
  "DM me · LAUNCH". The CTA is a comment keyword.
- **Ground:** dark, defocused 3D chrome cubes drift constantly behind the UI.

## Camera (measured)
- **Holds and pulls:** still on 33% of frames. Large zoom pulses: pull-outs of ×0.5 and ×0.22, push-ins of ×1.4–1.7.
- **Tilts:** a steady upward tilt (scrolling) from 4 s to 12 s, 460 px in one second at 10 s.
- **Roll:** the camera **rolls** and the planes **tilt in 3D** (|roll| > 0.3° on 14% of frames, up to 24° per second).
  This is unlike the user's flat camera rule. Learn the device; keep type and UI flat unless the user asks for tilt.
