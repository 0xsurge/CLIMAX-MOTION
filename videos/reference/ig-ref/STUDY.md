# Study: Lovable concept ad (Instagram, 30 s, 1280×720)

Contact sheets are `sheet.png` (2 fps) and the transition strips `t1.png` (5 s), `t2.png` (10.6 s) and `t3.png` (20.8 s).
The camera was measured with `camflow.py` (`flow.json`).

## Story (30 s)
1. **0–5 s, problem as a sentence.** "Getting an [glowing flower] idea is [icon] easy. Executing it? That's where everyone
   quits." Small dark type centred on a warm off-white ground. Icons and images sit inside the sentence, and a giant
   glossy 3D "?" floats in. The caret blinks after each line.
2. **5–6 s, the turn.** "quits" is pushed off by a bloom of pink and purple light. The light swells and collapses into
   the brand's heart mark on black, and the wordmark types beside it.
3. **6–9 s, the product.** The wordmark grows huge and fills the frame. The prompt box rises over it and the prompt
   types. The cursor clicks send, and the camera dives into the send button.
4. **9–13 s, the result.** One app screen on black explodes into dozens of real screens orbiting in depth, with
   cursors flying between them.
5. **13–20 s, real use.** A prompt pill on a gradient becomes the sign-in screen. The cursor fills it in and the
   button turns into a gradient success state. A toolbar pops out and its button grows.
6. **20–25 s, editing.** On a live web page, the cursor selects the headline, a box draws round it, the text
   retypes, and a font menu and colour picker open.
7. **25–30 s, close.** The cursor presses a "Publish" pill, a character pops out, it collapses to a dot, then the
   wordmark appears on black with the gradient light underneath.

## Measured
- **The camera is almost never still:** 7% of frames are near-still.
- **Fast reframes come in bursts.** The fastest is a sideways whip of about 3,700 px/s at 12.4 s. Zoom bursts reach
  +500 to +950%/s on dives (0.4, 5.2, 20.0, 27.6 s), and they are short, about 0.2–0.4 s.
- **Long sideways drifts** of 800–2,200 px/s carry the orbiting-screens section (12.4–14.2 s).

## Devices worth borrowing (styles, not copies)
1. **Images inside the sentence:** a small glowing icon sits between words ("Getting an [flower] idea").
2. **A giant glossy 3D symbol** floats behind the question, here a question mark.
3. **A light bloom carries the turn.** A soft colour cloud swallows the last word and condenses into the brand mark.
4. **The wordmark becomes the stage.** It scales up past the frame, and the product UI rises over it.
5. **Dive through the button.** The camera zooms into the clicked send button to reach the next scene.
6. **One screen bursts into many:** real screens scattered in depth, orbiting, with cursors travelling between them.
7. **A gradient success state.** The pressed button floods with the brand gradient and a check appears.
8. **Live editing as proof.** Select, retype, and pick a font and colour on a real page.
9. **A cursor that grows** into a glowing gradient arrow as it acts.
10. **Close.** Press, collapse to a dot, then the wordmark on black with the gradient light underneath.
