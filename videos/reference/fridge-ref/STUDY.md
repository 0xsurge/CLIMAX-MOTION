# Study: the fridge luma sweep (user reference, b.mp4; a.mp4 is a Gemini launch film sent with it)

The user pointed at the fridge moment in b.mp4 as the colour sweep to use (strips: fr1.jpg 8.6-10.6 s, fr2.jpg 29.2-31.0 s).

1. A glowing line of light rises from the floor to the top of the frame, about 0.6 s.
2. Below the line, the picture is gradient-mapped by its own brightness into a bright, high-key duotone (here blue/cyan/white).
   The objects take the colour: the black fridge turns pale blue. Darks are lifted, so nothing stays black.
3. Under that light the scene changes (warehouse to UI card, or crate to success card).
4. The new scene arrives in the same duotone and relaxes back into true colour over about 0.8 s, the floor first.

This is launch-style 4a / climax-launch D5 (the luma gradient sweep). Built for oraimo in videos/oraimo/neo/sweep_post.py.

## The film's structure (b.mp4, 71 s, retail software ad), studied for storyboard B
1. 0-2 s: one object on white, swapping (bean bag, lamp, fridge): a living slot.
2. 2-5 s: a real store, wide and clean; staff using a tablet POS (real photography).
3. 5-7 s: UI cards on a light blue ground (table, route map); the map becomes a real desert road with the van.
4. 8-10 s: the fridge in a warehouse with a floating "Remaining stock" tag; the luma sweep turns it into the brand world and into the product page.
5. 12 s: one big stat card ("7x refrigerators left").
6. 13-21 s: POS photo with floating stats; "Loyalty points" type into UI; a tilted UI plane with 3D glass triangles (the logo's shape) flying in depth.
7. 22-24 s: two icons (bank, POS) joined by a drawing line ("Financial reconciliation").
8. 25-31 s: a falling chart beside the real fridge; a crate in the warehouse, the luma sweep into a success card; a phone with the invoice.
9. 32-35 s: every scene converges into one collage ("in one second").
10. 36-50 s: logo and tagline; a stylised 3D world of stores; logos orbiting a centre badge.
11. 51-56 s: a real hand holding a phone at the till; the store front with people.
12. 57-71 s: the logo on a light brand gradient with the glass shards drifting.
