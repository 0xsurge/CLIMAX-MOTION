# Study: "Apple Store" product-card animation (Instagram, 8.6 s, 720x720, light ground)

One continuous carrier, no cuts, no camera moves: the objects do everything, on a pale grey-to-mint ground.
1. 0.0-1.0 s: the Apple logo assembles from flying fragments (spins in, settles).
2. 1.0-1.6 s: "apple" builds letter by letter while small bars wipe off the letters.
3. 1.6-2.6 s: a cluster of products flies in and gathers (laptop, phones, AirPods, Mac mini), turning slightly in 3D.
4. 2.6-3.1 s: the cluster collapses into a small white card; the card grows into a product card (AirPods Max, name, dropdown).
5. 3.1-6.4 s: the card fills with a colour (green, space grey, red, silver, blue) and the product inside swaps each time
   (each swap turns the product around, ~0.6 s per colour); swatch dots under it.
6. 6.4-7.7 s: the card widens into a spec sheet (H1 chip, ANC, transparency, spatial audio, lossless, Digital Crown) with a Buy pill.
7. 7.7-8.6 s: the sheet folds back to the product card, which shrinks to a dot.
Devices: one card as the carrier, swap inside a fixed frame (montage in a card), card morph into a spec sheet, collapse to a dot.
