Sterling: One key

One continuous take. Sterling's own key is the object we follow: it unlocks the green aurora, carries the story through the verification checks and the three steps, lands in the logo, and hands over to Mira. Every frame below is built from Sterling's and Mira's real logo, fonts, UI cards and illustrations, and every line is their own copy.
