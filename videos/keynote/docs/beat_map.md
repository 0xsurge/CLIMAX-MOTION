# Beat map: Armab keynote film (27 s, 120 BPM, 54 beats, 1440x1440)

Adapted from the brief to Armab's real story: the bento holds Armab projects, "Order print" is "Start a project", the delivered piece is a framed project print on the wall.

## Music (real track, edited on bar lines)

- **Electro Dreams** (Mixkit music id 190), measured **120.21 BPM** (time-stretched by 0.17% to exactly 120). Near-silent intro, clean drop at source bar 7 (+0.53 energy jump), about 15 bars of groove, a quiet breakdown at source bars 23-30, then the beat returns at bar 31.
- Edit (27.0 s = 13.5 bars): film bars 0-3 = source bars 3-6 (lead-in); film bars 4-11 = source bars 7-14 (groove, the drop is film beat 16); film bar 12 onward = source bars 23-24 (taper into the breakdown, under the Order and Wall beats); film beats 52-53 = the first two beats of source bar 31 (the beat returns as the letters land). Joins are on bar lines with a 20 ms equal-power crossfade.
- Loudness -14 LUFS after the mix. The intro is near-silent: the sound effects carry the first 8 seconds.

## Sound effects (downloaded, never synthesized)

45 Mixkit effects, listed in `audio/sfx_manifest.json` (ids, titles, URLs). Mixkit Sound Effects Free License: free for commercial use, no attribution, not redistributable on their own, so the audio files are git-ignored. Each cue is placed by its measured peak: the file's loudest transient is aligned to the visual contact frame, minus 30 ms.

## Notes

- Every scene is built out of the previous one: no fades, blurs or cuts.
- The last beat is tight: from beat 53 to 54 (0.5 s) the frame floods, contracts into the pill, the dot, and the letters spring back. If it feels rushed in the animatic I will pull the Order beats half a beat earlier.
- The day to golden hour pair is one photograph graded two ways (the site has no second shot of the same framing).
- The wall is a still crop of the site's corridor wall until real footage with moving shadows is supplied.
