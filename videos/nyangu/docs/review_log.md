## How the 12 principles are used
squash and stretch: every flying object stretches along its velocity, the badge, cards, chips and button squash on landing |
anticipation: the badge dips before it opens, pulses and wobbles while charging before the drop and the lockup |
staging: one focal action per beat; the focused step card takes the centre while the others recede |
straight ahead and pose to pose: letters are thrown on their own arcs; cards and windows move between keyed poses |
follow through and overlapping action: letters, cards, rows and strips are staggered and settle at different times |
slow in and slow out: closed-form springs only | arcs: every flight is a bezier arc |
secondary action: camera kicks and shake on hits, lime rings and pings, the rolling counters | timing: 4-8 frame snaps, short holds |
exaggeration: "space" opens its letter spacing, the Pay card gets a bigger hit | solid drawing: one light, consistent shadows |
appeal: the real brand.
