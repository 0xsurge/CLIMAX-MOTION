oraimo SpaceBuds Neo+ "Beyond the noise." - After Effects kit (Storyboard B)
For After Effects 2022 or newer.

1. Install the three fonts in /fonts (Inter Display Medium, SemiBold, Bold).
2. Keep build_spacebuds_neo.jsx next to the /footage and /audio folders.
3. After Effects: File > Scripts > Run Script File... > build_spacebuds_neo.jsx
4. Open the comp "MAIN SpaceBuds Neo+ Beyond the noise". The twelve scene comps (S00-S11) are in the Scenes folder.

In MAIN: the scenes are laid on one timeline with their transitions keyframed:
  2.1-2.75 s  push through the photo pill into Lagos
  6.0 s       light sweep (Tritone + two Linear Wipes on an adjustment layer, plus a rising light line)
  11.4 s      slide down to the walk
  16.0 s      pull out of the walk into its card (30 hours)
  19.4 s      slide down to the call; 23.4 s slide across to the gamer
  26.0 s      light sweep into the game
  27.4 s      slide up to the rain
  32.0 s      pull out of the rain into the collage
  35.4 s      slide across to the phone; 40.0 s slide up to the logo
Markers show every voice line. The audio is the voiceover (Kokoro voice 7) and the music with the sound effects.
The product images are oraimo's own renders (cut out); the photos are Unsplash License (see footage/photos/CREDITS.md).
If the repo's delivery folder sits next to this kit, the rendered film is added as a hidden guide layer.
