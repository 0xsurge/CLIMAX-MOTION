# Style guide: Armab Agency launch film (30 s, 120 BPM)

Everything below was measured from https://armabagency.vercel.app (see `assets/capture.json`), not guessed.

**Brand.** "Armab Agency — Strategic Brand & Digital Studio." Founder-led; "removing the roadblocks to your brand growth." Mark: a wooden hexagon with three growing plants (`brand-logo`), flat sage hexagon-and-bars icon (`logo-icon`), wordmark "Armab™" (`big-logo`). Hero wordmark is two-tone: "Armab" ink, "Agency" green.

**Palette (one accent family).** cream `#F3F3EF` canvas · ink `#14201F` text · forest `#0F2318` dark sections · ivory `#EDF1EB` text on dark · green `#64816D` (the accent) · sage `#B9CBB5` tint · card white. Hairline `#14201F` at 6% (the site draws faint vertical column lines on cream). No second hue; photography and client work bring their own color.

**Type.** Display: PP Neue Montreal 500, tight tracking (-0.03em), sentence case, huge (130-230 px on a 1440 site). Body and buttons: Aileron 400/600; buttons uppercase 600. Small labels: JetBrains Mono, uppercase. Fonts are the site's own files.

**Shape.** Buttons 5 px radius, ink fill, ivory text. White nav pill (ABOUT WORK [Armab™] SERVICES CONTACT). Project cards are plain images with a title and a mono "VIEW PROJECT" link. Testimonials are pastel pinned notes.

**Real material only.** Real screenshots at 2x of the nav pill, services list, project grid, testimonial notes and footer wordmark; the real logo files; the real project images (Fuhsi Market, Billet Design, Kitty, Opus). No redrawn UI. Copy is the site's own copy.

**Motion.** Closed-form springs only (snappy: buttons and leading edges; default: panels and camera; heavy: big type and logo). Type enters by masked rise, never by opacity alone. The camera moves through a continuous strip, never cuts or crossfades. Tiny overshoot on UI, none on type.

**Banned.** Centered title on a gradient, everything fading in, corner labels, glow, particle bursts, crossfades, spins/glitches/light leaks, bouncy easing on UI, dead time.

**Sound.** Synthesized warm bed (soft kick, felt hat, sub, marimba-like plucks) at 120 BPM; clicks on UI presses, soft thumps on the logo and CTA; cues placed from measured spring timings; -14 LUFS.

**Loop.** Last frame sets up the first frame.
