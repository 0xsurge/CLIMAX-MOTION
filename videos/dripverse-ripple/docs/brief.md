# DRIP VERSE "One order": brief
A beat-for-beat rebuild of the revest reference film ("One sale. A complete ripple.", 71.27 s, 16:9). It keeps the length, the 19 cut points
(each snapped to the nearest beat of the 120 BPM song), the 10-part story arc and the grammar: big serif statements, tilted UI planes,
floating 3D shapes that sweep the frame, glass stat cards, a hub of capabilities, and the shapes resolving into the logo. Nothing is copied
from revest: no logo, UI, footage, 3D renders or claims. Everything is DRIP VERSE's: bone #F3EFE8, ink #121214, brass #B08A4E, Bodoni Moda
for statements, Geist for UI and the wordmark, the brass drop as the floating shape (revest used triangles), and the user's shoe photos and cutouts.
