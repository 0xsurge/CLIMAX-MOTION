How I work / five steps

From idea to frame

Every brand gets a film of its own. The look is never reused; the process that finds it is.

  1. Find the truth

    One sentence for what the product does and for whom, and the tension it resolves.

  2. Pick one idea

    A single visual idea from the product's own world that can carry the story's turn.

  3. Build the colour story

    Colours chosen for what they mean in this film, tuned with colour theory and shown as moving light.

  4. Storyboard every beat

    Each line gets the device that says what the line says. You approve the boards before anything is built.

  5. Animate, score, refine

    A travelling camera, type that lands on the beat, sound designed around the visuals, and a frame-by-frame check.
