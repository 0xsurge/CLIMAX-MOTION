# Stage Orion: Cohort 1.0 internship recap (motion + interview edit)

Client: Stage Orion (stage-orion.com), Customer Support Internship, Cohort 1.0.
Status (2026-11-07): brief locked, waiting on intern footage folder.

## The three questions
1. How would you describe your experience during the Stage Orion Customer Support Internship?
2. What is the most important thing you learned during the internship that you will carry into your future career?
3. Be honest 😂 What was your biggest challenge or funniest moment during the internship?

## Structure (repeat per question)
1. **Question intro: motion design, style of Reference 2, but harder and faster.** Not slow.
   Question builds word by word (blur-in, flying punctuation crumbs), camera push, slot-machine
   word wheel with corner brackets, whip/blur transitions, dot-matrix dissolve surprise, cuts on beat.
2. **Intern answers: style of Reference 1.** Punch-in zooms, whoosh SFX, power-word bold captions
   with a glowing highlight box behind them. Cut each intern down to 1-2 sentences max.
   Match soundbites to questions by editorial feel; they don't have to be literal answers.
3. Bookend with Stage Orion logo + "COHORT 1.0".

Tools allowed: everything in .claude/skills (HyperFrames, Remotion, three.js, GSAP, motionmaxxing, etc.).

## Reference 1 (Sui x Samsung keynote edit, 60s, 16:9)
- Hook: payoff line ("IT IS INSANE") moved to the front, then 1.5s hard black.
- Arc: problem -> turn -> proof (count-up $49B->$80B, bar chart) -> announcement -> 5s logo card.
- Keyword captions, not subtitles: small lowercase lead-in + big caps punch word in blue box.
- Text placed behind the speaker's head (rotoscoped cutout).
- Slice/scramble letter reveals with a blue colour sweep. One accent colour.
- Something new every 1-2s on a single camera angle: PiP shrink, line icons, globe, data panels.
- Greyscale + fade to black on the low point, snap back to colour on the pivot.
- About -16 LUFS, steady dialogue-led mix.

## Reference 2 (Intercom "Fin" promo, 11.8s, 60fps)
- "you're still answering" builds word by word, blur-ins, flying crumbs (/ * 0 1).
- Camera push crops the sentence; last word becomes a slot-machine wheel
  (tickets -> emails -> chats -> DMs), blue corner brackets, motion blur, ~0.4-0.5s per word.
- Whip-blur into UI list, dot-matrix dissolve into sky/cloud world.
- Floating faces around "Ending up in just one inbox" (incl. a joke monkey photo).
- Blur is the transition language. One accent colour. Loud music (-7.8 LUFS), cuts on beat.
